About Us

PRIME BUILD CONSTRUCTION CHEMICALS is an ISO 14001:2018, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 9001:2015 certified company specialized in delivering high-end solutions for Waterproofing, Thermal Insulation, and Protective Coating systems.

Our advanced application systems are engineered for durability, rapid curing, and long-term structural protection even in the most demanding environments. We proudly serve industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects, delivering precision-applied solutions that outperform traditional coating technologies.

We are specialized in all forms of Spray & Injected Foam Insulation with a fully trained and fully approved workforce. Our systems are designed for Walls, Floors, and Internal & External Roof applications in both commercial and domestic projects.

Our mission is to provide innovative, environmentally responsible, and cost-effective construction chemical solutions that extend structural life, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance building performance across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
