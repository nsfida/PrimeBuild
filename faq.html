Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We Have Answers!

What services does Prime Build provide?

We specialize in Waterproofing, Thermal Insulation, and Protective Coating solutions for industrial, commercial, and residential projects.

Are your products ISO certified?

Yes, all our products and application systems comply with ISO 14001:2018, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 9001:2015 standards.

Do you offer on-site application?

Absolutely! Our fully trained and certified workforce provides on-site application for all systems including Spray & Injected Foam Insulation, Waterproofing, and Protective Coatings.

Can you handle both commercial and residential projects?

Yes, we handle projects of any scale including commercial, industrial, and residential buildings, offering solutions tailored to specific requirements.

Where are you located?

We are based in Dammam City, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, serving clients across the region.

How can I get a quote?

You can contact us via the Contact page or email us directly. We provide personalized quotes based on project scope and requirements.
